# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone.

**Expected:** be kind and constructive, assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
